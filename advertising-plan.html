📋 Step-by-Step Implementation Guide

WEEK 1: Free Foundation $0 COST

1 Optimize Google Business Profile ⭐ START HERE

Why: 70% of local searches happen on Google. This is FREE and crucial.

Action Steps:
  1. Go to google.com/business
  2. Claim/verify your business (takes 5-7 days for postcard verification)
  3. Complete 100% of your profile:
    • Add all phone numbers (610-869-3000, 302-235-1975, 484-643-2225)
    • Upload 10+ photos (team, trucks, before/after work)
    • Add all services (termite, bed bug, rodent control, etc.)
    • Set accurate business hours
    • Write detailed business description mentioning Philadelphia & Wilmington
    • Add service areas (Chester County, Delaware County, New Castle County)
  4. Get reviews immediately:
    • Text 10 recent happy customers for Google review
    • Businesses with 40+ reviews get 80% more traffic
    • Respond to every review within 24 hours

Time Required: 2 hours setup + ongoing maintenance

2 Claim Free Directory Listings

  • Yelp - biz.yelp.com
  • Nextdoor Business Page - business.nextdoor.com (FREE page, post twice/month)
  • Thumbtack - thumbtack.com/pro (pay per lead)
  • Angi/HomeAdvisor - List your services

Time Required: 3 hours total

WEEK 2: Nextdoor Ads Setup $50-100/month

Why: 77% of Nextdoor users are homeowners in your exact target neighborhoods. Extremely cost-effective.

Setup Steps:
  1. Create Business Account
    • Go to business.nextdoor.com/small-business
    • Sign up with business info
    • Verify your business
  2. Start with Local Deals (cheapest option)
    • Cost: $1-75 per neighborhood
    • Target specific Philly/Wilmington neighborhoods:
      • Northern Liberties, Northeast Philly, Fishtown (PA)
      • Pike Creek, Hockessin, Wilmington suburbs (DE)
  3. Create Your First Ad

Protect your [NEIGHBORHOOD] home with professional pest control.

✅ Licensed & Insured
✅ Same-Day Service Available
✅ 100% Satisfaction Guarantee

Call now: 610-869-3000
Special: $50 off first service (mention Nextdoor)
Budget Allocation:
  • Start with $50-75 total
  • Test 3-5 neighborhoods
  • Run for 2 weeks
  • Track which neighborhoods respond best

Time Required: 1 hour setup, 30 min/week monitoring

WEEK 3: Google Local Service Ads $100-200/month

Why: You ONLY pay when someone calls you. Appears at top of Google with "Google Guaranteed" badge.

⚠️ Note: Approval takes 2-4 weeks, so start this process early!
Setup Steps:
  1. Gather Required Documents First:
    • Business license (PA & DE pest control license)
    • Insurance certificates (general liability, workers comp)
    • Owner & employee background check info
  2. Sign Up:
    • Go to ads.google.com/local-services-ads
    • Click "Get Started"
    • Enter business info
  3. Complete Profile:
    • Business name: Absolute Pest Services
    • Service areas: Select specific ZIP codes
      • Philadelphia area: 19390, 19348, 19382 (West Grove/Chester County)
      • Wilmington area: 19707, 19810, 19803 (Hockessin/New Castle County)
    • Select services: Termites, Bed Bugs, Rodents, Ants, General Pest Control
    • Add business hours
    • Upload photos
  4. Google Guaranteed Screening:
    • Submit license documents
    • Complete background checks
    • Wait 2-4 weeks for approval
  5. Set Budget:
    • Start with $150-200/week budget
    • Let Google auto-optimize your bids initially
    • You'll only pay when someone actually contacts you
  6. Once Live:
    • Respond to leads within 5 minutes (massive ranking boost)
    • Download Google LSA mobile app for instant notifications
    • Dispute spam/invalid leads for refunds

Time Required: 2 hours setup + 2-4 weeks approval wait

WEEK 4: Social Media Presence FREE + Optional Ads $50-100/month
