## 3. Interlinking Strategy

### Link Architecture
```
Homepage (root)
├── Service Pages (linked from homepage services section)
│   ├── /wildlife-control
│   ├── /bed-bug-treatment
│   ├── /termite-treatment
│   └── /bat-removal
│
├── Service Area Pages (linked from homepage + service pages)
│   ├── /service-areas/chester-county-pa
│   ├── /service-areas/delaware-county-pa
│   ├── /service-areas/new-castle-county-de
│   ├── /service-areas/montgomery-county-pa
│   └── /service-areas/northeast-maryland
│
└── General Service Areas Hub
    └── /service-areas
```

### Link Flow Rules
1. **Homepage → Service Pages:** Services section cards should link to individual service pages
2. **Homepage → Area Pages:** Service areas section should link to county-specific pages
3. **Service Pages → Area Pages:** Each service page should mention and link to relevant county pages
4. **Area Pages → Service Pages:** Each county page should list and link to all services
5. **Footer Links:** All pages should have footer links to key service and area pages

### Anchor Text Best Practices
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text (not "click here")
- Examples:
  - "Learn about our [wildlife control services](/wildlife-control) in Chester County"
  - "[Schedule termite inspection](/termite-treatment) for your Kennett Square home"
  - "We serve [Delaware County PA](/service-areas/delaware-county-pa) including Media and Newtown Square"
