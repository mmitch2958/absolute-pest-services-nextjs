📝 Step 2: SendGrid Domain Authentication

2Authenticate Your Domain in SendGrid

Once logged into SendGrid:

  1. Click "Settings" in the left sidebar
  2. Click "Sender Authentication"
  3. Under "Domain Authentication", click "Authenticate Your Domain" (blue button)
  4. You'll be asked a series of questions:
    • "Which DNS host do you use?" Select "Other Host (Not Listed)"
    • "Would you like to brand the links for this domain?" Select "Yes"
  5. Enter your domain: absolutepestservices.com
  6. Click "Next"
  7. IMPORTANT: SendGrid will now display DNS records you need to add. Keep this page open!
📋 What You'll See:

SendGrid will generate 3-4 DNS records that look like this:

  • 2 CNAME records for DKIM (email signing)
  • 1 CNAME record for mail subdomain
  • Sometimes 1 TXT record for SPF

Do NOT click verify yet! First, add the records to Cloudflare.

☁️ Step 3: Add DNS Records to Cloudflare

⚠️ CRITICAL: Add DNS records ONLY in Cloudflare, NOT in GoDaddy. Your domain's DNS is managed by Cloudflare.

3Access Cloudflare DNS Settings

  1. Log into your Cloudflare account at https://dash.cloudflare.com
  2. Click on your domain: absolutepestservices.com
  3. In the left sidebar (or top menu), click "DNS" → "Records"
  4. You should see a list of existing DNS records

4Add SendGrid DKIM Records

For each CNAME record that SendGrid provided (usually 2):

  1. Click "Add record" button in Cloudflare
  2. Fill in the details exactly as shown in SendGrid:
    Field What to Enter
    Type CNAME
    Name Copy from SendGrid (e.g., s1._domainkey)
    Target Copy from SendGrid (e.g., s1.domainkey.u12345.wl.sendgrid.net)
    TTL Auto
    Proxy status DNS only (gray cloud ☁️, NOT orange 🟠)
  3. Click "Save"
  4. Repeat for the second DKIM record
⚠️ IMPORTANT: The proxy status MUST be set to "DNS only" (gray cloud). If it's orange, click it to toggle to gray.

5Add Mail Subdomain CNAME (if provided)

If SendGrid gave you a third CNAME record for linking:

  1. Click "Add record" again
  2. Type: CNAME
  3. Name: Copy from SendGrid (often em1234 or similar)
  4. Target: Copy from SendGrid
  5. TTL: Auto
  6. Proxy: DNS only (gray cloud)
  7. Click "Save"

6Add SPF Record

Check if you already have an SPF record:

  • Search your Cloudflare DNS records for a TXT record with name @
  • Look for a value starting with v=spf1

If you DON'T have an SPF record:

  1. Click "Add record"
  2. Fill in:
    Field Value
    Type TXT
    Name @
    Content v=spf1 include:sendgrid.net ~all
    TTL Auto
  3. Click "Save"

If you ALREADY have an SPF record:

  1. Click "Edit" on the existing SPF record
  2. Add include:sendgrid.net before the ~all or -all
  3. Example: Change from v=spf1 ~all to v=spf1 include:sendgrid.net ~all
  4. Click "Save"
📌 What is SPF?

SPF (Sender Policy Framework) tells email providers that SendGrid is authorized to send emails on behalf of your domain.

7Add DMARC Record

DMARC is critical for Microsoft 365 deliverability:

  1. Click "Add record"
  2. Fill in:
    Field Value
    Type TXT
    Name _dmarc
    Content v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:rob@absolutepestservices.com; pct=100; adkim=r; aspf=r
    TTL Auto
  3. Click "Save"
📌 DMARC Policy Explanation:
  • p=none - Monitor mode (won't reject emails, just collects data)
  • rua=mailto:rob@absolutepestservices.com - You'll receive daily reports
  • adkim=r; aspf=r - Relaxed alignment (recommended for SendGrid)

After a few weeks of monitoring, you can change p=none to p=quarantine for stronger protection.

✅ Step 4: Verify in SendGrid

8Complete Verification

  1. Go back to the SendGrid tab you kept open
  2. Wait 5-10 minutes after adding DNS records (to allow initial propagation)
  3. Click the "Verify" button in SendGrid
  4. SendGrid will check your DNS records
✓ Success: You should see green checkmarks next to each DNS record. Your domain is now authenticated!
⚠️ If Verification Fails:
  • Double-check you copied the DNS records exactly (no extra spaces)
  • Verify Cloudflare proxy is set to "DNS only" (gray cloud) for CNAME records
  • Wait 15-30 minutes and try verifying again
  • DNS can take up to 48 hours to fully propagate globally

🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues

Issue: SendGrid Verification Still Pending

Solutions:

  • Wait 15-30 minutes and try again
  • Check that you entered DNS records exactly as shown (no typos)
  • Verify CNAME records have proxy status "DNS only" in Cloudflare
  • Use this tool to check DNS propagation: whatsmydns.net

Issue: Emails Still Going to Spam After 48 Hours

Possible Causes:

  • Reputation Building: Microsoft needs 3-7 days to trust your domain
  • Content Issues: Avoid spam trigger words in subject lines (FREE, URGENT, !!!)
  • Engagement: Ask recipients to mark your emails as "Not Junk" and add to contacts
  • Shared IP Reputation: SendGrid's shared IPs can be affected by other users' spam

Issue: Mail-Tester Score Below 8

Check These:

  • SPF: Should show "PASS" (check your SPF record includes sendgrid.net)
  • DKIM: Should show "PASS" (verify DKIM CNAME records are correct)
  • DMARC: Should show "PASS" (check your DMARC TXT record)
  • Review the detailed report on mail-tester.com for specific issues
